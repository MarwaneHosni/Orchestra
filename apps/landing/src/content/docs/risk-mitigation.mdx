# Risk Mitigation

Orchestra identifies potential risks during blueprint generation and provides mitigation strategies.

## Risk Types

### Technical Risks

- **Scope creep**: Unclear or expanding requirements
- **Technology mismatch**: Chosen stack doesn't fit requirements
- **Integration complexity**: Too many external dependencies
- **Performance bottlenecks**: Insufficient scalability planning

### Business Risks

- **Budget overrun**: Underestimated costs or timeline
- **Market fit**: Unclear value proposition
- **Compliance**: Missing regulatory requirements
- **Security**: Insufficient security measures

### Operational Risks

- **Team capacity**: Skills or resources not available
- **Deployment complexity**: Insufficient DevOps planning
- **Maintenance burden**: No long-term maintenance strategy
- **Data management**: Missing backup/recovery plans

## Mitigation Strategies

When risks are identified, Orchestra:
1. **Flags** the risk in the blueprint
2. **Suggests** specific mitigation actions
3. **Adjusts** the task plan to account for risk handling
4. **Provides** execution prompts with risk-aware implementation

## Handling Risks

After generation, you can:
1. Review the **Risks** section in the blueprint
2. Click **"Mitigate"** on individual risks
3. Provide additional context or constraints
4. Regenerate the affected sections with risk-aware prompts

## Example

A risk like ""Unproven AI integration"" might be mitigated with:
- Fallback to manual processing if AI fails
- A/B testing framework for AI features
- Gradual rollout with feature flags
- Monitoring and alerting for AI performance
